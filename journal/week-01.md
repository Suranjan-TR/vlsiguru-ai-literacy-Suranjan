## One AI output I initially trusted

I initially trusted AI-generated explanations because they were presented clearly and confidently.

During my SystemVerilog experiment, I found that different AI tools could give different answers to the same technical question. This showed me that a confident answer is not always a correct answer.

## How I verified it

I checked important claims against reliable technical sources and compared the information with the material provided in the course.

I also compared answers from different AI tools and independently checked the result when possible.

## What the AI did well

AI helped me understand unfamiliar concepts, organize information and provide examples quickly. It was especially useful when I was learning new AI concepts and needed simple explanations.

## What the AI could not be trusted to decide

I should not rely on AI alone for important technical facts, experimental results, specifications, or other claims that require evidence.

These should be checked using reliable sources, calculations, experiments, or appropriate technical documentation.

## One question I still have
