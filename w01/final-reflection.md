## Final Reflection

### 1. What is the most useful thing you learned this week?

The most useful thing I learned is that AI is a tool that can help me learn, solve problems, and work with information, but I should not blindly trust its answers. I learned that verification is important, especially for technical and engineering information.

### 2. What did you initially misunderstand?

I initially misunderstood the difference between AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI. I also thought that an answer from an AI assistant was likely to be correct if it sounded confident.

### 3. What did AI help you do?

AI helped me understand AI concepts in simple language, compare different concepts, generate examples, and explore how AI can be useful in Design Verification and other VLSI-related tasks.

### 4. Where did you need to verify or correct AI?

I needed to verify AI when I tested the SystemVerilog question about the default values of `bit`, `logic`, and `int`. ChatGPT gave an incorrect value for `int`, while other AI assistants gave the correct answer. I checked the answer using a technical reference and corrected the result.

How can AI tools be practically used in VLSI Design Verification without blindly depending on AI-generated code or answers?
